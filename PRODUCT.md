# PennyWise

A local personal finance tracker for a first-time Java developer. The user enters income and expenses manually, organizes them by category, and reviews a monthly summary. They use personal accounts in different countries; until currency support exists, the interface must make the single-currency limitation clear. Data belongs to the authenticated user. The product runs locally and should remain understandable and easy to operate. Visual preferences are unspecified; a calm, accessible, practical interface is assumed.
